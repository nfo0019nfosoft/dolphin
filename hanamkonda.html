ABOUT OUR HOSPITAL

Dolphin Women & Children Hospital – Hanumakonda, is dedicated to providing advanced Neonatology, Pediatrics, and Pediatric Surgery services with compassionate and specialized care for newborns and children. Our Neonatology department offers expert care for premature babies and critically ill newborns with modern NICU facilities and continuous monitoring. The Pediatrics department focuses on the complete health and development of infants, children, and adolescents through preventive care, vaccinations, diagnosis, and treatment. Our Pediatric Surgery team provides safe and advanced surgical care for children using modern techniques and child-friendly treatment approaches to ensure faster recovery and better outcomes. With experienced specialists, advanced technology, and a caring environment, Dolphin Hospital is committed to delivering trusted healthcare for every child and family.
