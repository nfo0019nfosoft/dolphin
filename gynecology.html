GYNECOLOGY

Home › Services ›Gynecology

MENSTRUAL DISORDERS TREATMENT

We provide comprehensive care for menstrual disorders including irregular periods, heavy bleeding, missed cycles, and painful menstruation. Our approach involves detailed hormonal evaluation and diagnostic assessments to identify the root cause. Personalized treatment plans help regulate cycles, reduce discomfort, and improve overall reproductive health and quality of life.

PCOS / PCOD MANAGEMENT

Our PCOS/PCOD management focuses on balancing hormones and addressing symptoms like irregular periods, acne, and weight gain. We offer a combination of medical treatment, lifestyle guidance, and dietary support. Fertility counseling is also provided for women planning pregnancy. Long-term monitoring ensures better hormonal stability and improved overall well-being.

THYROID & HIGH BP IN WOMEN

We specialize in managing thyroid disorders and high blood pressure that can affect women’s reproductive and overall health. Regular monitoring, lab evaluations, and medication management are provided to maintain optimal levels. Our care also focuses on lifestyle modification and preventive strategies to reduce long-term complications.

WHITE DISCHARGE TREATMENT

We offer accurate diagnosis and treatment for abnormal vaginal discharge caused by infections, hormonal imbalance, or underlying conditions. Our care includes lab testing, targeted medication, and preventive guidance. Early treatment helps avoid complications and ensures comfort, hygiene, and reproductive health.

UTERINE FIBROIDS TREATMENT

We provide advanced care for uterine fibroids with both medical and minimally invasive treatment options. Our goal is to reduce symptoms such as heavy bleeding, pelvic pain, and pressure while preserving fertility whenever possible. Individualized care plans ensure effective and safe management.

OVARIAN CYST DIAGNOSIS & TREATMENT

Our experts diagnose and manage ovarian cysts using advanced imaging and clinical evaluation. Most cysts are treated conservatively, while complex cases may require surgical intervention. We focus on preventing complications and maintaining healthy ovarian function and fertility.

ENDOMETRIOSIS TREATMENT

Specialized treatment for endometriosis includes pain management, hormonal therapy, and surgical options when needed. We aim to reduce chronic pelvic pain and improve fertility outcomes. Early diagnosis and consistent follow-up help manage symptoms effectively and improve quality of life.

PELVIC PAIN EVALUATION

Chronic pelvic pain can have multiple causes, and we provide comprehensive evaluation to identify the exact issue. Diagnostic imaging, lab tests, and clinical assessment are used to create a targeted treatment plan. Our goal is long-term relief and improved daily functioning.

CERVICAL SCREENING (PAP SMEAR)

Pap smear screening helps detect early changes in cervical cells and prevents cancer progression. We recommend routine screening for early diagnosis and timely intervention. Safe and quick procedures ensure minimal discomfort and maximum preventive benefit.

BREAST EXAMINATION & SCREENING

We provide clinical breast exams and screening to detect lumps, infections, and early signs of breast cancer. Regular checkups help ensure early detection and better treatment outcomes. Awareness and preventive care are key to maintaining breast health.

VAGINAL INFECTIONS / UTI TREATMENT

We treat urinary tract infections and vaginal infections with accurate diagnosis and effective medication. Our approach includes identifying underlying causes and providing preventive care guidance. Timely treatment prevents recurrence and complications.

HORMONAL IMBALANCE TREATMENT

Hormonal imbalance can affect mood, weight, cycles, and fertility. We provide detailed evaluation and customized treatment plans to restore hormonal balance. Lifestyle counseling and medical therapy ensure long-term stability and health.

MENOPAUSE MANAGEMENT

We support women during menopause with symptom management, hormone therapy, and lifestyle advice. Our care focuses on managing hot flashes, mood changes, and bone health. Regular monitoring helps maintain comfort and long-term wellness.

INFERTILITY EVALUATION & TREATMENT

Our fertility services include detailed evaluation of both partners, ovulation tracking, and advanced treatment options. We guide couples through every step of their fertility journey with personalized care and emotional support. Our goal is successful and healthy conception.

UROGYNECOLOGY

We specialize in treating pelvic floor disorders, urinary incontinence, and bladder issues affecting women. Our care includes advanced diagnostics, physiotherapy, and surgical options when required. The goal is to restore comfort, control, and quality of life.

Trusted healthcare with advanced technology and compassionate care.
