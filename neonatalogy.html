NEONATOLOGY

Home › Services ›Neonatology

Level IV / Level III NICU (Neonatal Intensive Care Unit)

Our advanced Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) is equipped to manage critically ill newborns with continuous monitoring and life-saving support systems. We provide specialized care using modern technology and expert neonatologists to ensure the highest standards of safety and survival for newborns.

PREMATURE & LOW BIRTH WEIGHT BABY CARE

We provide specialized care for premature and low birth weight babies with advanced monitoring, temperature control, and nutritional support. Our team ensures proper growth and development through continuous care and expert supervision during the most delicate stages of life.

24/7 DM NEONATOLOGIST & EMERGENCY CARE

Our highly qualified neonatologists are available round-the-clock to handle emergencies and critical newborn conditions. Immediate response, expert diagnosis, and advanced treatment ensure timely care and better outcomes for newborn babies in urgent situations.

VENTILATOR & CPAP SUPPORT FOR NEWBORNS

We provide advanced respiratory support including ventilators and CPAP systems for newborns with breathing difficulties. Our NICU ensures precise oxygen delivery and continuous monitoring to stabilize babies and support healthy lung development.

NEONATAL INFECTIONS MANAGEMENT

Early diagnosis and prompt treatment of infections in newborns are critical for survival. We provide specialized infection management using advanced diagnostics and targeted therapies to prevent complications and ensure safe recovery.

Management of Neonatal Jaundice (Phototherapy & Exchange Transfusion)

We treat neonatal jaundice using advanced methods such as phototherapy and exchange transfusion when required. Continuous monitoring ensures safe bilirubin levels and prevents complications, helping babies recover quickly and safely.

SEPSIS & INFECTION MANAGEMENT

Neonatal sepsis requires immediate attention and intensive care. Our team provides rapid diagnosis, antibiotic therapy, and supportive care in a controlled environment. Early intervention significantly improves survival rates and long-term health outcomes.

DEVELOPMENTAL FOLLOW-UP FOR HIGH-RISK BABIES

We offer long-term follow-up care for high-risk newborns to monitor growth, neurological development, and overall health. Regular assessments help detect delays early and ensure timely intervention for better developmental outcomes.

NEONATAL NUTRITION & BREASTFEEDING SUPPORT

Proper nutrition is essential for newborn growth and immunity. We provide lactation support, feeding guidance, and nutritional care for both term and preterm babies. Our goal is to ensure healthy weight gain and strong immunity from the early stages of life.
