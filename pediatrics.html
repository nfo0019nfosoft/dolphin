PEDIATRICS

Home › Services ›Pediatrics

24/7 PEDIATRIC EMERGENCY CARE

Our pediatric emergency services are available round-the-clock to handle urgent medical conditions in children. We provide rapid diagnosis, immediate treatment, and continuous monitoring with experienced specialists. Our goal is to ensure timely care and complete safety during critical situations.

ADVANCED PICU

Our Pediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU) is equipped with advanced technology to manage critically ill children. Continuous monitoring, specialized equipment, and expert pediatric intensivists ensure the highest level of care for life-threatening conditions and recovery.

GENERAL PEDIATRIC CONSULTATION

We offer comprehensive consultations for infants and children, covering routine checkups, illness diagnosis, and preventive care. Our pediatricians provide personalized treatment plans to support healthy growth, development, and long-term well-being.

VACCINATION & IMMUNIZATION PROGRAMS

We provide complete vaccination programs following recommended schedules to protect children from preventable diseases. Our team ensures safe administration, parental guidance, and timely reminders for every stage of a child’s immunization journey.

TREATMENT FOR FEVER & COMMON ILLNESSES

We provide expert treatment for fever, infections, and common childhood illnesses. Accurate diagnosis and timely medication help ensure quick recovery. Our approach focuses on comfort, safety, and prevention of complications in children.

PEDIATRIC NUTRITION & DIET COUNSELING

Proper nutrition is essential for growth and immunity in children. We provide personalized diet plans based on age and health needs. Our guidance helps establish healthy eating habits and supports optimal physical and cognitive development.

PEDIATRIC INFECTIOUS DISEASE CARE

We specialize in diagnosing and treating complex infections in children. Our care includes advanced testing, targeted therapy, and continuous monitoring. Early intervention ensures faster recovery and reduces the risk of complications.

GROWTH & DEVELOPMENT MONITORING

Regular monitoring of physical growth and developmental milestones helps ensure children progress normally. We assess height, weight, behavior, and cognitive skills. Early detection of delays allows timely intervention and better long-term outcomes.

PEDIATRIC SKIN & ALLERGY TREATMENTS

We treat skin conditions such as rashes, eczema, and allergies in children with safe and effective methods. Our care focuses on identifying triggers, reducing symptoms, and preventing recurrence for better skin health and comfort.

PEDIATRIC PULMONOLOGY (ASTHMA & LUNG CARE)

We provide specialized care for asthma and respiratory conditions in children. Advanced diagnostics and treatment plans help manage breathing difficulties. Our goal is to improve lung function and ensure better quality of life.

PEDIATRIC NEUROLOGY

Our neurology services focus on diagnosing and managing conditions like seizures, developmental delays, and neurological disorders. We provide comprehensive care with advanced evaluation and long-term monitoring to support healthy brain development.

PEDIATRIC CARDIOLOGY

We provide specialized care for congenital and acquired heart conditions in children. Our services include diagnosis, monitoring, and treatment planning. Early detection ensures better outcomes and improved heart health for growing children.

PEDIATRIC GASTROENTEROLOGY

We diagnose and treat digestive disorders including feeding issues, abdominal pain, and gastrointestinal conditions. Our care focuses on improving digestion, nutrition, and overall health through tailored treatment and dietary guidance.

PEDIATRIC SURGERIES

We offer safe and advanced surgical care for children with expert pediatric surgeons and modern facilities. From minor to complex procedures, we ensure precision, safety, and faster recovery with complete post-operative care and support.

Trusted healthcare with advanced technology and compassionate care.
