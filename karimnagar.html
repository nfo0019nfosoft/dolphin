ABOUT OUR HOSPITAL

Dolphin Women & Children Hospital – Karimnagar Branch is a trusted destination for comprehensive women and child healthcare services, offering expert medical care with compassion and commitment. Our hospital is equipped with modern infrastructure, advanced diagnostic technology, and experienced specialists dedicated to providing personalized treatment for every patient. We specialize in Gynecology, Obstetrics, Pediatrics, Neonatology, infertility care, pregnancy care, and women’s wellness services, ensuring complete healthcare support from maternity to child development. With a patient-centered approach and a focus on safety, comfort, and quality treatment, Dolphin Hospital continues to serve families across Karimnagar and surrounding regions with excellence.

Painless Delivery (Epidural Labour Support)

At Dolphin Women & Children Hospital, we provide advanced painless delivery services designed to make childbirth safer, smoother, and more comfortable for mothers. With the support of experienced obstetricians, anesthesiologists, and dedicated maternity specialists, epidural analgesia helps reduce labour pain while allowing mothers to remain conscious, relaxed, and actively involved during delivery.

Reduces labour pain and delivery discomfort
Allows mothers to stay alert during childbirth
