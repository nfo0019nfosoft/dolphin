ABOUT OUR HOSPITAL

Dolphin Women & Children Hospital – Karimnagar Branch is a trusted healthcare destination dedicated to providing advanced healthcare services for women, newborns, infants, children, and adolescents with compassionate and specialized medical care. Our hospital is designed to create a safe, comfortable, and patient-friendly environment where every mother and child receives personalized attention with warmth and care. Equipped with modern infrastructure, advanced NICU facilities, maternity care support, pediatric intensive care services, and state-of-the-art diagnostic technology, Dolphin Hospital ensures high-quality treatment for women’s health, pregnancy care, newborn care, and child health conditions. Our experienced team of gynecologists, obstetricians, neonatologists, pediatricians, pediatric surgeons, nurses, and healthcare professionals work together to deliver ethical, transparent, and patient-centered care at every stage of treatment and recovery. We specialize in pregnancy care, maternity services, normal and cesarean deliveries, newborn intensive care, child health checkups, vaccinations, growth and developmental monitoring, pediatric emergency care, and advanced pediatric surgical procedures using modern medical techniques. With a strong commitment to safety, clinical excellence, and compassionate healthcare, Dolphin Hospital continues to serve families across Karimnagar and surrounding regions with trust, dedication, and excellence in women and child healthcare services.
