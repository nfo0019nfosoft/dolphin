OBSTETRICS

Home › Services ›Obstetrics

ROUTINE PREGNANCY CHECK-UPS

Regular pregnancy check-ups are essential to monitor the health of both mother and baby throughout the pregnancy journey. We provide scheduled visits, vital assessments, and necessary screenings to track progress. Early detection of any issues ensures timely care and a safe pregnancy outcome with continuous medical guidance.

HIGH-RISK PREGNANCY CARE

Our high-risk pregnancy care is designed for mothers with conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, or previous complications. We provide specialized monitoring, advanced diagnostics, and expert consultation. Continuous supervision helps reduce risks and ensures a safer delivery for both mother and baby.

ANTENATAL (PRENATAL) CARE

Comprehensive antenatal care includes regular health check-ups, nutritional guidance, and fetal monitoring. We ensure early screening of complications and provide education for a healthy pregnancy. Our goal is to support mothers with complete care and preparation for childbirth and postnatal recovery.

ECTOPIC PREGNANCY MANAGEMENT

Ectopic pregnancy requires early diagnosis and immediate medical intervention. Our team uses advanced imaging and expert care to manage such cases safely. Prompt treatment helps prevent serious complications and ensures the health and safety of the patient.

NORMAL DELIVERY

We encourage safe and natural vaginal deliveries with expert obstetric support and modern facilities. Our team ensures comfort, continuous monitoring, and emergency readiness during labor. A patient-centered approach helps create a positive and safe birthing experience.

PAINLESS / EPIDURAL DELIVERY

Epidural delivery provides effective pain relief during labor while maintaining safety for mother and baby. Our specialists administer controlled anesthesia to reduce discomfort. This allows mothers to experience childbirth in a calm and comfortable manner.

CESAREAN SECTION (C-SECTION)

We perform both planned and emergency C-sections with advanced surgical techniques and strict safety protocols. Our experienced team ensures smooth procedures and quick recovery. Post-surgical care is also provided for optimal healing and comfort.

POSTNATAL CARE

Postnatal care focuses on the recovery of the mother and the health of the newborn. We provide guidance on breastfeeding, nutrition, emotional well-being, and newborn care. Regular follow-ups ensure a smooth transition into motherhood.

FETAL GROWTH & HEALTH MONITORING

Continuous monitoring of fetal growth ensures proper development throughout pregnancy. We use advanced techniques to track baby’s health, movement, and growth patterns. Early detection of issues allows timely medical intervention and better outcomes.

FETAL ULTRASOUND SCANS

Ultrasound scans provide detailed insights into fetal development, position, and health. We perform routine and advanced scans to monitor progress and detect abnormalities early. Safe imaging ensures accurate diagnosis without risk.

PREGNANCY COMPLICATION MANAGEMENT

We manage complications such as gestational diabetes, preeclampsia, and infections with expert care. Our team ensures close monitoring and timely treatment. Proper management reduces risks and supports a safe pregnancy journey.

POST-DELIVERY RECOVERY SUPPORT

We provide complete support for recovery after childbirth including physical healing, nutrition, and emotional care. Guidance on breastfeeding and newborn care is also included. Our aim is to ensure a healthy and smooth recovery period.

24/7 EMERGENCY OBSTETRIC CARE

Our emergency obstetric services are available round-the-clock for urgent pregnancy situations. Equipped with advanced facilities and expert doctors, we ensure immediate care and quick response. This helps safeguard both mother and baby during critical times.

VBAC (VAGINAL BIRTH AFTER CESAREAN)

We offer safe VBAC options for eligible women with previous cesarean deliveries. Careful monitoring and expert supervision ensure safety during labor. This approach supports natural childbirth while minimizing surgical risks.

PAINLESS DELIVERY (WATER BIRTHING / EPIDURAL ANALGESIA)

We provide advanced painless delivery options including water birthing and epidural techniques. These methods help reduce labor pain and improve comfort. Mothers experience a relaxed and controlled birthing process.

ADVANCED FETAL DOPPLER & ANOMALY SCANS

Advanced Doppler and anomaly scans help assess fetal organs, blood flow, and structural development. These scans detect abnormalities early and guide medical decisions. Accurate diagnosis ensures better pregnancy outcomes.

Trusted healthcare with advanced technology and compassionate care.
